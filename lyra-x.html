Privacy & lifecycle

A processing service, not a storage library.

No audio library

Raw audio is not retained as a permanent library. It is removed after processing.

24-hour window

Standard transcript artifacts are available for 24 hours, then deleted permanently.

Download in time

Download your work before it expires — recovery isn't possible after the window closes.
