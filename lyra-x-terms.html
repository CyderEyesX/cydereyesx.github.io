2. Description of Service

LYRA-X is a privately-hosted, subscription-based transcription service operated by CyderEyesX LLC. The Service converts uploaded, pre-recorded audio into a speaker-labelled, timestamped transcript using WhisperX (large-v3) with GPU acceleration and speaker diarization.
