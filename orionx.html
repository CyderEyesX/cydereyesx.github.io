What you get

Honest about its limits

Text chat in beta

The current offer describes no message cap; this does not mean unlimited compute or guaranteed availability. Existing plan terms remain unchanged.

🔒 Private infrastructure

Runs on hardware we own and control. Your conversations are not sold or used to train third-party models.

📅 Knowledge cutoff, disclosed

The model's knowledge has a training cutoff date. We tell you exactly what that is — no surprises.

🪙 Beta seats, genuinely limited

We cap active seats to keep response times reasonable on our current hardware.

🚫 No web search (yet)

The Basic tier does not browse the internet. That's coming in a future tier.

🚫 No image generation (yet)

Text conversation only for now. Image generation is on the roadmap.

Planned — not released

A future accessibility companion.

The goal is clear, guided tasks, not stronger model intelligence claims. Future LYRA-X workflows would use only transcripts you authorize. Future AccessX Tap workflows would respect account and label permissions.

Voice, read-aloud responses and connected workflows require engineering, consent and user testing. They are not in the current text beta. Web browsing and image generation are not currently offered.
