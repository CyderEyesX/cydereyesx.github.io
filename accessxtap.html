How it works

Print Braille. Tap. Understand.

  1. 1. Upload a document, audio clip, video, or type a description in your dashboard.
  2. 2. AccessX Tap generates a short, secure link.
  3. 3. Write the link to an NFC tag and print your Braille label on it.
  4. 4. A student taps the tag with any phone and instantly hears or reads the content — no app, no download.
