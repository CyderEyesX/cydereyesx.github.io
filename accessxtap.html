FAQ

Common questions about accessible NFC content

How do I make printed materials accessible for blind or low-vision students?

Upload the document, audio, or video to AccessX Tap, generate a short secure link, write it to an NFC tag, and add a Braille label. A student taps the tag with any phone and instantly hears or reads the content — no app, no download.

What is an NFC accessibility label?

A small NFC tag paired with a Braille or large-print label. Tapping it opens screen-reader-ready content — audio, video, or clean text — hosted on AccessX Tap.

Do students need to install an app?

No. Tapping the tag opens the content in the phone's browser and works with the built-in screen reader (VoiceOver / TalkBack).

Is AccessX Tap screen-reader compatible and accessibility-focused?

Yes. Text renders as clean pages screen readers read natively, and PDFs, audio and video play inline. It is built specifically for blind and low-vision access.

How much does accessible NFC content hosting cost?

Plans start at $49/month for a department (up to 250 tags) and $199/month for a whole campus. Pilots are available by request.

Can museums, libraries, or businesses use AccessX Tap — not just schools?

Yes. Any organization that wants tap-to-access, accessible content — exhibits, signage, handouts — can use it.
